Narrowing down

Comfortaa 700 · per-TLD color system

Wordmark 02 wins: Comfortaa 700. Below, the same wordmark and its favicon shown as a system across three TLDs, each with its own accent color: .com — moss green, .nl — warm orange, .dev — steel blue. Six treatments per TLD (solid, circle, outline, two-tone, reversed, with-period), plus a system view.

Ink centroid verification

Six test shapes with known analytical centroids, rasterized through the same measurement pipeline used on the favicons. Green ✓ means the measurement agrees with the analytical value within ± 1 pixel (in 128×128 canvas units). If any test fails, the favicon centering is suspect. The m. test also proves why the period feels visually unbalanced — the centroid is mathematically correct, but the small isolated glyph pulls the eye disproportionately.
