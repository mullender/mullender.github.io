Wordmark · ten fonts × TLDs

Exploring the wordmark

You gravitated to concept 10 for its rounded m. Rounded doesn't come from Helvetica — it comes from the font choice. Below are ten wordmark treatments across fonts with different personalities, paired with different TLDs (.com, .nl, .dev) so you can hear how each combination sounds. Each mark is shown large on cream, mid-size on dark, and small at nav size — that last one is where most wordmarks live in the wild.

After you pick a font family, next tweaks: try tighter tracking, a custom ligature between l+l, or a two-tone TLD where the dot is one color and the letters another. Also worth trying: lowercase-only with a full-stop period vs an en-dash separator (mullender—nl).
