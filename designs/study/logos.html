Sketch phase

Logo concepts for mullender.com

Ten directions in the spirit of Allan Peters' logo process: research the brand, word-associate, crank out lots of small thumbnails (quantity over polish), cull the strongest, then vector and refine. Each concept is shown on cream and on moss, plus a 32 / 16 px favicon preview so you can see which marks survive at tab-size.

Brand words

Engineer · problem-solver · AI-augmented · embedded · home automation · archer · hiker · Dutch · physicist · forest · understated.

Palette

01. Slab M

The safest, most universal concept — a bold geometric M in a rounded slab. Reads clean at 16 px, works on any surface. Baseline to measure the more distinctive ideas against.

02. Broadhead

A single broadhead arrow tip. Pure archery. Bold silhouette holds at any size and doubles as a directional cursor — nice for a favicon.

03. Bowdrawn

An M formed by two drawn bows with an arrow shaft cutting through the middle. Reads as M and as archery at once. Gets fussy small; the favicon strips down to just the bow silhouettes.

04. Antler Range

The Hoh-rainforest elk and the hiker overlap: silhouette reads simultaneously as mountain peaks and antler tines, with a subtle M valley cut through the center. Personal without being literal.

05. Gable House

Two Amsterdam stepped canal-house gables, side by side. Reads as an M silhouette, a nod to Dutch heritage, and a house shape for home automation. Steps collapse to peaks at the 16 px favicon.

06. Trace

An M drawn as a single PCB copper trace with plated vias at each terminus. Nods to the embedded work. Monoline weight scales cleanly by adjusting stroke width.

07. Orbit

An M with a satellite tracing an orbital path around it. Physicist and AI reference — the orbit line disappears at favicon size, leaving M plus a dot. Might be too abstract.

08. Home Node

A house silhouette with a wifi/signal arc emanating from a dot. On the nose for home automation. Loses the wifi at favicon size, becoming a generic house — probably too generic to carry the brand.

09. Trailhead Seal

A circular badge combining mountain range + arrow + wordmark curved around the rim. Full lockup for a bio card or a laptop sticker. Simplifies well to mountains-in-circle at favicon size.

10. Wordmark

Type-only, tight tracking, a colored period. Echoes the lowercase "also:" line on the site and doesn't try to be clever. Favicon falls back to a solid lowercase m in a slab.

Next step (Peters again): pick 2–3 favorites, refine the vectors (custom letterforms rather than system-ui, tighter geometry), test in the real header, and see which one still feels right after a day away from it.
